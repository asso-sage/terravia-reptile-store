## 🦎 About TERRAVIA

**TERRAVIA** is a premium concept store dedicated to terrariums, reptile habitats, and specialized accessories.

Our goal is to bring together carefully selected products for reptile keepers who want to create environments that combine functionality, natural aesthetics, comfort, and thoughtful habitat design.

From complete terrarium setups to lighting, heating, substrates, hides, climbing accessories, humidity equipment, feeding supplies, and decorative elements, TERRAVIA is designed as a complete destination for reptile habitat solutions.

The brand combines a **natural visual identity**, premium presentation, and a modern shopping experience inspired by the environments reptiles naturally inhabit.

---

## 🌿 Our Concept

TERRAVIA is built around one simple idea:

> **Create a better habitat by bringing nature closer to home.**

The store focuses on products that help transform ordinary enclosures into carefully designed environments.

Our visual identity combines:

- 🌿 Botanical elements
- 🪵 Natural wood textures
- 🪨 Earth-inspired materials
- 🦎 Reptile-inspired details
- 🌱 Deep botanical greens
- ✨ Premium bronze accents
- 🖤 Dark modern backgrounds

This combination gives TERRAVIA a distinctive identity without making the store feel generic or overly artificial.

## 🖼️ Image Guidelines

Product photography is an important part of the TERRAVIA experience.

Images should follow these principles:

- Realistic appearance
- Clean presentation
- Individual product framing
- Centered composition
- Product fully visible
- Minimal distractions
- Consistent proportions
- High visual quality
- No unnecessary interface elements
- No prices embedded inside images
- No buttons or website screenshots inside product images

The goal is to make every product look like a real premium catalog item.
